\textbf{Veto verdict} (Kavach): any single minister returning \textsc{Block}
vetoes the call unconditionally; no score averaging or learned aggregation.

adding a structural rule; the cosine-triage corpus by adding a natural-language
attack-pattern description, re-embedded without retraining.
