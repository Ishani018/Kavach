Table~\ref{tab:guardrail-comparison} extends \S\ref{sec:related}'s summary
to all 13 systems given their own row (plus 13 more name-checked in a
footnote for space), so a reader can see the full landscape at once rather
than reconstructing it from scattered prose citations. Rows are grouped by
enforcement boundary --- input-time, plan-time, tool-call-time,
information-flow, drift-detection, and red-team/scanning --- matching
\S\ref{sec:background}'s taxonomy, so the boundary a system enforces at is
visible directly from its position in the table, not just its ``Boundary''
column. As stated in the caption, AgentDojo ASR and InjecAgent Recall are
each system's own best reported number, not independently reproduced here;
the table is a map of mechanisms and enforcement points, not a
leaderboard, since benchmark subset, environment version, and threat model
all vary system to system and no number in it is directly comparable
across rows.
